What to write about

  • Corrections — if a benchmark looks wrong for your market, or you've spotted an error in an article, tell us. Include the page and what you think it should say. We'd rather fix it than defend it.
  • The calculator — bugs, a cost line you wish it included, or a format it doesn't handle well. Most improvements have come from operators asking for them.
  • Article requests — if there's a number you keep needing and can't find a straight answer on, that's usually a good sign it should be written up.
  • Advertising — put "Advertising" in the subject line.
  • Privacy — data requests go to privacy@restaurantprofitcalc.com. See the privacy policy for what we do and don't hold.

What we can't help with

We can't review your accounts, advise on a lease, or tell you whether to open a second site. Those decisions need someone who knows your business, your market, and your tax position — take them to a qualified accountant. This site is a starting point, not a substitute for advice.

RestaurantProfitCalc.com is a free tool for restaurant and hospitality operators. Results are indicative only and do not constitute financial advice.
